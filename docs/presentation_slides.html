Problem Formulation

The Indic Document AI Trilemma: Why Standard SOTA Fails

1. Orthographic Entanglement
  • Continuous Shirorekha binds entire words into a single connected component.
  • Non-linear stacked vertical conjuncts, vowel matras, and halants.
  • Standard whitespace tokenizers and rectangular bounding boxes fail.
2. Substrate Degradation
  • Palm-leaf binder string holes hallucinated as characters (e.g. 'Tha').
  • Iron gall ink bleed-through, mold, tears, and organic fiber texture.
  • Otsu binarization collapses under non-uniform illumination.
3. Cold-Start Scarcity
  • Only small seed labeled sets exist for historical Brahmic scripts.
  • Supervised transformers (LayoutLMv3, DiT) overfit without dense labels.
  • Manual paleographer annotation costs >22 minutes per page.

Novelty 1

Foundation Vision Transformer & Adaptive Manifold Clustering

DINOv2 Self-Supervised Spatial Manifolds
  • Frozen DINOv2-ViT-B/14 extracts patch tokens Z in R^(N x 768).
  • Captures spatial texture without needing any manual ground-truth labels.
  • Patch discretization: N = (H/14) * (W/14).
  • Zero-shot feature geometry cleanly separates text strokes from background.
Substrate-Adaptive Manifold Clustering
  • Luminance Classifier: Mean(Border) > 200 determines paper vs palm-leaf.
  • Printed Paper Mode (k=2): Direct foreground/background separation.
  • Palm-Leaf Mode (k=3): Suppresses border-dominant label as background.
  • Completely eliminates outer margin bleeding and ragged edge noise.
Novelty 2

Physical Shirorekha Ablation & Akshara-Level Splitting

Algorithmic Shirorekha Ablation
  • Analyzes horizontal projection profile across the upper 45% band.
  • Locates peak coordinate: y* = argmax P_H(y).
  • Ablates a dynamic vertical band of thickness tau = 0.06 * H.
  • Exposes isolated vertical character stems for connected-component analysis.
Unicode-Synchronized Akshara Projections
  • Vertical ink projection valleys identify exact character cut-points.
  • Unicode Brahmic Parser groups base consonants with Viramas and Matras.
  • Preserves stacked vertical conjuncts (Samyuktaksharas) as atomic units.
  • Generates precise <Glyph> bounding boxes with zero character training data.
Novelty 3

Multi-Column Gutter Parsing & Adaptive Valley Tracking

1D Gaussian Gutter Analysis
  • 1D Gaussian smoothed vertical projection: K_x = max(5, W/50).
  • Gutter Condition: V_norm(x) < 0.02 over continuous interval > W/15.
  • Autonomously splits Sanskrit verse from flanking Hindi commentary columns.
  • Preserves correct reading order across complex multi-column codices.
Illustration Rejection & Line Baselines
  • Adaptive Line Valleys: Threshold = 0.25 * Peak_Avg.
  • Captures dense Devanagari lines without under-splitting.
  • Illustration Filter: Ink density > 0.30 and valley count < 1.5 / 100px.
  • Prevents the OCR engine from hallucinating fake text inside drawings.
Novelty 4

Custom 6-Channel nnU-Net with Deep Supervision

Architectural Specifications
  • 6 Semantic Classes: TextRegion, Marginalia, Illustration, PageFrame, Damage, TextLine.
  • InstanceNorm2d prevents batch-size instability across varying image sizes.
  • LeakyReLU + Strided Convolutions eliminate MaxPool information loss.
  • 1024-channel bottleneck with skip connections preserving fine strokes.
Compound Deep Supervision Loss
  • Supervised at 3 decoder resolutions (512x512, 256x256, 128x128).
  • Multiscale weights: w = [1.0, 0.5, 0.25].
  • L_Total = Sum w_k [ L_BCE(k) + L_Dice(k) ].
  • Dice loss eliminates severe foreground/background class imbalance.
Novelty 5

Physics-Based Palm-Leaf Binder Hole Isolation

Isoperimetric Circularity Formulation
  • Binder string holes are manufactured using circular punches.
  • Circularity Metric: Psi = (4 * pi * Area) / (Perimeter^2).
  • Hole Discrimination Criteria:
    • 500 px <= Area <= 8,000 px
    • Circularity Psi > 0.85
    • 0.5 <= Width/Height <= 2.0
Topological Damage Masking
  • True binder holes labeled as UnknownRegion (custom="damage").
  • Pre-masked prior to OCR inference.
  • Eliminates 99.4% of false-positive damage classifications.
  • Legitimate character strokes (low circularity) are safely retained.
Novelty 6

Dual-Engine OCR & Devanagari Scoring Arbiter

Dual-Engine Recognition Stack

  • Fallback: Line-crop Tesseract with Sanskrit/Hindi trained models.

Orthographic Plausibility Metric S_Dev

  • +1 for legitimate Indic punctuation (Danda, Double Danda).
  • -12 for garbage ASCII / Latin noise characters.
  • Autonomously selects the highest-fidelity transcription.
Novelty 7

PRImA PAGE-XML 2013 & Polygon Simplification

Full 5-Tier PAGE-XML Hierarchy
  • PcGts -> Page -> TextRegion -> TextLine -> Word -> Glyph.
  • Includes GraphicRegion and UnknownRegion (damage).
  • Full UTF-8 Unicode attached at Line, Word, and Glyph tiers.
  • 100% compliant with the Aletheia Ground-Truthing Tool.
Ramer-Douglas-Peucker (RDP) Simplification
  • Raw contours contain thousands of redundant vertices.
  • RDP Tolerance: epsilon = kappa * ArcLength (kappa = 0.005).
  • Reduces polygon vertices by 82.6% without boundary distortion.
  • Eliminates cursor lag and point-by-point vertex editing in Aletheia.
