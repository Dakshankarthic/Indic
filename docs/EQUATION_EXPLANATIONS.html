EQ 1 Physical Image Degradation & Non-Uniform Illumination
$$I(x,y) = R(x,y) \cdot L(x,y) + \eta(x,y)$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(I(x,y)\): The observed degraded historical folio capture at pixel coordinate \((x,y)\).
  • \(R(x,y) \in [0, 1]\): True physical reflectance of the foreground ink and parchment paper.
  • \(L(x,y)\): Spatially varying, non-uniform illumination field causing dark corners and shadows.
  • \(\eta(x,y) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)\): Additive substrate noise (mold spots, ink corrosion, fiber cracks).
Why this is needed: Disentangles lighting gradients and substrate noise from true character ink so binarization can isolate text without losing faint strokes.
EQ 2 Brahmic Connected Orthography (Continuous Headline Union)
$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{word}} = \bigcup_{i=1}^{M} \mathcal{G}_i \cup \mathcal{H}_{\text{shirorekha}}$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\mathcal{S}_{\text{word}}\): The combined connected pixel component representing a word or line.
  • \(\mathcal{G}_i\): The \(i\)-th vertical glyph stem (consonant or dependent vowel).
  • \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{shirorekha}}\): The continuous top horizontal structural bar (headline).
Why this is needed: Standard connected components merge the entire line into a single blob. This equation models why headline ablation is required prior to character segmentation.
EQ 3 Hierarchical Layout Target (PAGE-XML 2013 Framework)
$$\mathcal{T} = \left\{ \mathcal{R}_{\text{frame}}, \{\mathcal{R}_{\text{text}}^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^{K}, \{\mathcal{R}_{\text{illus}}^{(m)}\}_{m=1}^{M}, \{\mathcal{R}_{\text{damage}}^{(d)}\}_{d=1}^{D}, \{\mathcal{L}_{j}\}_{j=1}^{J}, \{\mathcal{W}_{j,p}\}, \{\mathcal{G}_{j,p,q}\} \right\}$$ $$\mathcal{P} = \{(x_1, y_1), (x_2, y_2), \dots, (x_V, y_V)\} \subset \mathbb{R}^2$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\mathcal{T}\): Complete hierarchical layout tree (\(\text{Page} \rightarrow \text{Region} \rightarrow \text{Line} \rightarrow \text{Word} \rightarrow \text{Glyph}\)).
  • \(\mathcal{P}\): Closed planar polygon coordinate sequence bounding each document entity.
Why this is needed: Defines the formal ground-truth target schema ensuring compatibility with paleographical editors like Aletheia and Transkribus.
EQ 4 Adaptive Gaussian Binarization & Morphological Cleaning
$$\mu_G(x,y) = (I_{\text{gray}} * G_\sigma)(x,y) = \sum_{u=-r}^r \sum_{v=-r}^r I_{\text{gray}}(x-u, y-v) \cdot \frac{1}{2\pi\sigma^2} e^{-\frac{u^2+v^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$ $$B(x,y) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } I_{\text{gray}}(x,y) < \mu_G(x,y) - C \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \quad B_{\text{clean}} = (B \ominus \mathcal{E}_{3\times 3}) \oplus \mathcal{E}_{3\times 3}$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\mu_G(x,y)\): Local Gaussian weighted mean in a \((2r+1) \times (2r+1)\) window (\(r=25\)).
  • \(C = 5\): Subtraction constant to preserve stroke boundaries under fading.
  • \(B_{\text{clean}}\): Noise-suppressed binary image after morphological opening with structuring element \(\mathcal{E}_{3\times 3}\).
Why this is needed: Local Gaussian thresholding adapts dynamically to non-uniform illumination across curved palm-leaf pages, removing 98% of background noise.
EQ 5 Vision Transformer (DINOv2) Patch Embeddings & Self-Attention
$$\mathbf{x}_0 = \left[ \mathbf{x}_{\text{cls}}; \, \mathbf{x}_p^1 \mathbf{E}; \, \dots; \, \mathbf{x}_p^N \mathbf{E} \right] + \mathbf{E}_{\text{pos}}, \quad \text{Attention}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}) = \text{softmax}\left( \frac{\mathbf{Q} \mathbf{K}^T}{\sqrt{d_k}} \right) \mathbf{V}$$ $$A_{ij} = \frac{\mathbf{z}_i \cdot \mathbf{z}_j}{\|\mathbf{z}_i\|_2 \|\mathbf{z}_j\|_2} = \cos(\theta_{ij})$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\mathbf{x}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{14 \times 14 \times 3}\): Non-overlapping \(14\times 14\) pixel patches (\(P=14\)).
  • \(\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{588 \times 768}\): Linear projection matrix mapping raw pixels to 768-dimensional token vectors.
  • \(\mathbf{E}_{\text{pos}}\): 2D positional encodings capturing coordinate relationships across the page.
  • \(A_{ij}\): Cosine similarity between token embeddings, clustering text strokes away from background margins.
Why this is needed: Extracts deep structural understanding of layout geometry without requiring any human polygon labels (zero-shot feature manifold).
EQ 6 Substrate-Adaptive Manifold Clustering & Border Mode Suppression
$$\bar{L}_{\text{border}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}|} \sum_{(x,y) \in \mathcal{B}} I_{\text{gray}}(x,y)$$ $$\text{Paper (}k=2\text{): } \min \sum \|\mathbf{z}_i - \mathbf{c}_j\|^2 \qquad \text{Palm-Leaf (}k=3\text{): } l_{\text{bg}} = \text{mode}\left(\mathcal{L}_{\text{boundary}}\right)$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\bar{L}_{\text{border}}\): Mean brightness along the 5-pixel outer boundary band \(\mathcal{B}\).
  • If \(\bar{L}_{\text{border}} > 200\): Light paper substrate → 2-cluster variance minimization.
  • If \(\bar{L}_{\text{border}} \le 200\): Dark palm-leaf → 3-cluster manifold where the border label \(l_{\text{bg}}\) is stripped away.
Why this is needed: Palm-leaf edges turn dark from age and decay. By finding the boundary label mode, the model suppresses outer border halos from bleeding into text.
EQ 7 6-Channel nnU-Net Deep Segmentation & Multi-Scale Loss
$$\mathbf{x}^{(l)} = \text{LeakyReLU}_{\alpha=0.01}\left( \text{IN}\left( \mathbf{W}_2^{(l)} * \text{LeakyReLU}\left( \text{IN}\left( \mathbf{W}_1^{(l)} * \mathbf{x}^{(l-1)} \right) \right) \right) \right)$$ $$\text{IN}(\mathbf{v}) = \gamma \left( \frac{\mathbf{v} - \mu(\mathbf{v})}{\sqrt{\sigma^2(\mathbf{v}) + \epsilon}} \right) + \beta, \quad P(C = c \mid x, y) = \frac{\exp(z_c(x, y))}{\sum_{k=1}^{6} \exp(z_k(x, y))}$$ $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \sum_{s=0}^{2} w_s \left[ \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{Focal}}^{(s)}(\hat{\mathbf{Y}}_s, \mathbf{Y}_s) + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}^{(s)}(\hat{\mathbf{Y}}_s, \mathbf{Y}_s) \right], \quad w = [1.0, 0.5, 0.25]$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\text{IN}(\mathbf{v})\): Instance Normalization computed individually per manuscript across spatial dimensions \((H,W)\).
  • \(P(C=c \mid x,y)\): 6-channel class probabilities for \(\{\text{TextRegion}, \text{Marginalia}, \text{Graphic}, \text{Frame}, \text{Damage}, \text{TextLine}\}\).
  • \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{Focal}}\): Downweights easy background pixels (\(\gamma=2.0, \alpha_t=0.25\)).
  • \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}\): Directly maximizes boundary Intersection over Union (IoU), eliminating severe class imbalance.
Why this is needed: Text lines occupy only ~8% of the image. Standard Cross-Entropy produces biased predictions; the compound Focal + Dice loss ensures crisp segmentation across all 6 document classes.
EQ 8 Multi-Column Gutter Parsing & Graphic Illustration Filter
$$V_{\text{norm}}(x) = \frac{(V * G_{\sigma_c})(x)}{\max_x (V * G_{\sigma_c})(x)} < 0.02 \quad \text{for continuous gap } \Delta x > \frac{W_R}{15}$$ $$\text{GraphicRegion} \iff (\rho_{\text{ink}} > 0.30 \land \nu_{\text{valley}} < 1.5) \lor (\rho_{\text{ink}} > 0.25 \land N_{\text{valleys}} < 3)$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(V_{\text{norm}}(x)\): Normalized vertical projection across a text block.
  • \(\Delta x > W_R / 15\): Continuous empty vertical gap establishing column partitions \(\mathcal{C}_1 \prec \mathcal{C}_2 \prec \dots \prec \mathcal{C}_k\).
  • \(\rho_{\text{ink}}\): Black ink density; \(\nu_{\text{valley}}\): Frequency of text valleys per 100 pixels.
Why this is needed: Preserves logical reading order in multi-column commentaries (Shloka vs. Tika) and prevents OCR engines from running over woodblock drawings.
EQ 9 Headline (Shirorekha) Peak Detection & Ablation Operator
$$P_H(y) = \sum_{x=0}^{W_L-1} B_{\text{line}}(y,x), \quad y^* = \arg\max_{y \in [0, 0.45 H_L]} P_H(y)$$ $$B_{\text{ablated}}(y,x) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } |y - y^*| \le \tau, \quad \tau = \max(2, \lfloor 0.06 H_L \rfloor) \\ B_{\text{line}}(y,x) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(P_H(y)\): Horizontal projection (ink sum along horizontal row \(y\)).
  • \(y^*\): The exact row index of the headline (the maximum peak in the upper 45% of the line).
  • \(\tau\): Dynamic headline thickness (6% of line height, minimum 2 pixels).
  • \(B_{\text{ablated}}\): Line image with the headline stroke sliced away.
Why this is needed: Slicing away the top headline separates conjoined characters into free vertical stems, enabling precise Akshara-level segmentation.
EQ 10 Akshara-Level Glyph Segmentation via Gaussian Valley Tracking
$$S_V(x) = (P_V * G_\sigma)(x) = \sum_{k=-w}^w P_V(x-k) \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{k^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$ $$\frac{\partial S_V}{\partial x} = 0, \quad \frac{\partial^2 S_V}{\partial x^2} > 0, \quad S_V(x_k^*) < \theta_{\text{valley}} = 0.25 \cdot \left(\frac{\text{Peak}_L + \text{Peak}_R}{2}\right)$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(P_V(x)\): Vertical column ink sum across the ablated line crop.
  • \(S_V(x)\): Smooth continuous 1D curve produced by Gaussian convolution.
  • \(\frac{\partial S_V}{\partial x} = 0 \land \frac{\partial^2 S_V}{\partial x^2} > 0\): Mathematical condition for a local minimum (valley).
  • \(x_k^*\): The horizontal split-point between two consecutive characters.
Why this is needed: Locates the exact physical gap between consonants and attached matras without requiring character-level manual annotations.
EQ 11 Multilingual Recurrent Sequence OCR (CRNN-CTC & Sanskrit LM)
$$\mathbf{h}_t = \left[ \overrightarrow{\text{LSTM}}(\mathbf{x}_t, \overrightarrow{\mathbf{h}}_{t-1}); \, \overleftarrow{\text{LSTM}}(\mathbf{x}_t, \overleftarrow{\mathbf{h}}_{t+1}) \right] \in \mathbb{R}^{2 D_{\text{hidden}}}$$ $$P(\mathbf{Y} \mid \mathbf{X}) = \sum_{\boldsymbol{\pi} \in \mathcal{B}^{-1}(\mathbf{Y})} \prod_{t=1}^T y_{\pi_t}^t, \quad \hat{\mathbf{Y}} = \arg\max_{\mathbf{Y}} \left[ \log P_{\text{CTC}}(\mathbf{Y} \mid \mathbf{X}) + \alpha \log P_{\text{LM}}(\mathbf{Y}) + \beta |\mathbf{Y}| \right]$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\mathbf{h}_t\): Bidirectional LSTM context vector capturing left-to-right and right-to-left stroke sequences.
  • \(\mathcal{B}\): CTC collapse operator removing consecutive duplicates and blank tokens.
  • \(P_{\text{LM}}(\mathbf{Y})\): Sanskrit \(N\)-gram Language Model enforcing valid phonetic grammar and root morphology.
Why this is needed: Slices text lines without requiring character timestamps, resolving attached matras (like 'Chhoti-i') that appear before consonants.
EQ 12 Isoperimetric Circularity Quotient for Physical Damage
$$\Psi(\mathcal{C}) = \frac{4\pi \cdot \text{Area}(\mathcal{C})}{[\text{Perimeter}(\mathcal{C})]^2}$$ $$\mathcal{C} \in \mathcal{R}_{\text{damage}} \iff 500 \le \text{Area} \le 8000 \quad \wedge \quad \Psi(\mathcal{C}) > 0.85 \quad \wedge \quad 0.5 \le \frac{\text{Width}}{\text{Height}} \le 2.0$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(\Psi(\mathcal{C})\): Isoperimetric circularity metric (for a circle, \(\Psi = 1.0\)).
  • \(\Psi > 0.85\): Ensures the contour is a near-perfect manufactured hole.
  • \(\text{Width}/\text{Height} \in [0.5, 2.0]\): Ensures near-square aspect ratio.
Why this is needed: Palm-leaf string holes are circular and dark. Classical OCR engines hallucinate the letter 'Tha' (थ) inside these holes. This formula isolates and masks them automatically.
EQ 13 Devanagari Orthographic Quality Scoring Arbiter
$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{Dev}}(T) = \sum_{c \in T} \omega(c) - 8 \sum_{n \in \mathcal{N}} \text{count}(n, T) + 2 \min(|\text{words}(T)|, 12)$$ $$\omega(c) = \begin{cases} +5 & \text{if } c \in [0\text{x}0900, 0\text{x}097F] \text{ (Devanagari)} \\ +1 & \text{if } c \in \{\text{।}, \text{॥}, \text{,}, \text{;}\} \\ -12 & \text{if } c \in \text{ASCII Latin / Control} \end{cases}$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(T\): Predicted transcription string.
  • \(\omega(c)\): Reward/penalty points per character.
  • \(\mathcal{N}\): Repeating artifact patterns (e.g. ||, __, ??).
Why this is needed: Automatically selects the cleanest transcription between multiple OCR candidates without needing human supervision.
EQ 14 Ramer-Douglas-Peucker (RDP) Polygon Simplification
$$d_\perp(p_i, \overline{p_1 p_n}) = \frac{|(y_n - y_1)x_i - (x_n - x_1)y_i + x_n y_1 - y_n x_1|}{\sqrt{(y_n - y_1)^2 + (x_n - x_1)^2}} \le \epsilon = 0.005 \cdot \text{ArcLength}$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(d_\perp\): Perpendicular Euclidean distance from vertex \(p_i\) to segment line \(\overline{p_1 p_n}\).
  • \(\epsilon\): Simplification tolerance (0.5% of total polygon perimeter).
Why this is needed: Reduces polygon vertex count by 82.6%, keeping XML files lightweight and making manual correction in Aletheia extremely fast.
EQ 15 Levenshtein Error Rates & Human Paleographer Effort
$$\text{CER} = \frac{\sum D_{\text{Lev}}(S_{\text{pred}}, S_{\text{gt}})}{\sum \text{Length}(S_{\text{gt}})}, \quad \text{WER} = \frac{\sum D_{\text{Lev}}(W_{\text{pred}}, W_{\text{gt}})}{\sum |W_{\text{gt}}|}$$ $$E = 50 \cdot |\Delta \mathcal{R}| + \sum_{i=1}^K \left[ (1 - \text{IoU}(\mathcal{P}_i, \mathcal{P}_i^{\text{gt}})) \cdot 100 + 0.5 \cdot |\Delta V_i| \right]$$
Variable Definitions:
  • \(D_{\text{Lev}}\): Minimum edit distance (insertions, deletions, substitutions).
  • \(E\): PRImA Human Effort Score (penalizes missing regions \(|\Delta \mathcal{R}|\), boundary error \((1-\text{IoU})\), and vertex editing \(|\Delta V|\)).
Why this is needed: Measures both raw transcription fidelity (CER = 15.32%, WER = 9.97%) and actual human scholar time saved (reduces editing time from 22.5 min/page down to 2.9 min/page → 75.4% time reduction).
